Requirement Intelligence Portal

Turn business intent into compliant, execution-ready prompts for Autonomous AI Agents.

Describe what you want to change. Upload supporting material if you have it. The platform analyzes business context, technical impact, compliance guardrails and risks, then compiles a secure, execution-ready initialization script for AI coding agents.

Two Entry Points

Business Mode — Start from a business need

For business stakeholders, product owners, transformation teams and CIO offices.

  • "We need to improve customer onboarding"
  • "We must comply with a new regulation"
  • "The current approval process is too slow"
  • "We want to launch a new digital service"
  • "Customers are dropping off during document upload"

IT Mode — Set Execution Boundaries

For application owners, architects, engineering teams and IT demand managers. Define the guardrails AI agents must respect.

  • Define allowed repositories and access scopes
  • Set mandatory architectural patterns (e.g., Event-driven, Microservices)
  • Establish risk thresholds and CI/CD gates
  • Inject enterprise tech-stack constraints

Multimodal Input Support

The platform accepts multiple types of input:

  • Text: Describe the business outcome, process issue, regulation, or technical problem.
  • Audio: Upload audio notes or meeting recordings (mp3, wav, m4a).
  • Video: Upload meeting recordings, demos, walkthroughs or screen recordings (mp4, mov, webm).
  • Images: Upload screenshots, whiteboards, process diagrams, mockups, error screens (png, jpg, webp).
  • Documents: Upload business documents, policies, regulations, BRDs, functional specs (pdf, docx, txt, md).
  • URLs: Add URLs to applications, documentation pages, or business references.
  • Repositories: Connect a repository or technical source as supporting evidence.

Guided Intake Questions

Business Mode

  1. What business outcome are you trying to achieve?
  2. Who is impacted by this change?
  3. What is not working today?
  4. What would success look like?
  5. Is there any deadline, regulation, risk or dependency we should consider?

IT Mode

  1. Which application, service or repository should be assessed?
  2. What change, issue or modernization goal should be considered?
  3. Are there known dependencies or constraints?
  4. Are there security, compliance or architectural standards to respect?
  5. What output do you need: requirements, user stories, technical analysis, effort estimate or delivery plan?

Agentic Analysis

Multiple agents analyze the input in parallel:

  1. Business Intent Agent: Extracts objectives, stakeholders, pain points, value drivers and success metrics.
  2. Process Analysis Agent: Identifies impacted business processes, user journeys and operational gaps.
  3. Technical Impact Agent: Analyzes repositories, application structure, APIs, dependencies and system constraints.
  4. Risk & Compliance Agent: Maps regulatory, security, privacy, resilience and operational risks.
  5. Requirement Generation Agent: Creates functional requirements, technical requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.
  6. Delivery Planning Agent: Produces implementation options, effort estimate, dependencies, test strategy and handoff package.
  7. Compliance & Security Guardrail Agent: Automatically maps business intent against GDPR, DORA, and internal security policies to inject non-negotiable architectural constraints.

Output: Requirement Intelligence Pack

The platform generates a comprehensive requirement pack with 10 sections:

1. Executive Summary

Business objective, current problem, expected outcome, impacted stakeholders, value hypothesis, urgency/deadline, confidence score.

2. Business Requirements

Business capabilities impacted, process changes needed, user groups affected, success metrics, business rules, open questions.

3. Functional Requirements

Requirement ID, description, user role, priority, rationale, acceptance criteria.

4. Enterprise Guardrails (Shift-Left Security)

Non-negotiable compliance constraints automatically injected from GDPR, DORA, and internal security policies. Each guardrail includes a trigger, enforcement action, regulatory standard reference, and severity level.

5. Technical Requirements

Systems impacted, APIs/integrations, data implications, architecture considerations, non-functional requirements, security and compliance constraints.

6. User Stories

Format: "As a [user], I want [capability], so that [business outcome]." Each story includes priority, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risks, suggested test cases.

7. Risk & Dependency Map

Technical dependencies, business dependencies, compliance risks, delivery risks, data risks, operational risks.

8. Delivery Plan

Recommended implementation path, MVP scope, out-of-scope items, estimated complexity, suggested delivery phases, test strategy, rollback considerations.

9. Compiled Devin Initialization Script (Primary Output)

The primary export: a compiled Agent_Init_Script.md containing Context & Goal, Strict Boundaries (injected from compliance), and Acceptance Criteria as Executable Tests.

10. Evidence Analysis

Extracted insights from uploaded audio, video, images, documents, and repositories.

Demo Scenario: Improve Digital Customer Onboarding

Business input: "Our customer onboarding journey has high drop-off during document upload and manual approval. We want to reduce abandonment, accelerate approval time and improve compliance controls."

Executive Summary

  • Business Objective: Reduce customer onboarding drop-off and accelerate approval time while maintaining compliance standards.
  • Current Problem: High abandonment during document upload (35% drop-off), slow manual approval process (avg 3 days), no real-time status visibility for customers or operations.
  • Expected Outcome: Onboarding completion rate above 90%, approval time under 4 hours, zero compliance gaps, full audit trail and operational visibility.
  • Impacted Stakeholders: Retail customers, Operations team, Compliance officers, Customer success managers, IT engineering team.
  • Confidence Score: 87%

Functional Requirements

  1. FR-001: Allow customers to resume incomplete onboarding applications (Priority: High)
  2. FR-002: Improve document upload with drag-and-drop, mobile camera capture, and real-time validation (Priority: High)
  3. FR-003: Add real-time onboarding status visibility for customers (Priority: High)
  4. FR-004: Implement automated routing of applications based on risk score (Priority: High)
  5. FR-005: Add SLA monitoring dashboard for operations team (Priority: Medium)
  6. FR-006: Notify operations when SLA thresholds are breached (Priority: Medium)
  7. FR-007: Generate compliance audit trail for all onboarding decisions (Priority: High)
  8. FR-008: Automate document validation for standard KYC document types (Priority: Medium)

User Stories

  • US-001: As a retail customer, I want to resume my incomplete onboarding application, so that I don't have to start over if I get interrupted.
  • US-002: As a retail customer, I want clear feedback when my document upload fails, so that I can fix the issue and complete onboarding.
  • US-003: As an operations team member, I want to see a dashboard of pending applications with SLA status, so that I can prioritize my review queue.
  • US-004: As a compliance officer, I want to generate audit reports for all onboarding decisions, so that I can demonstrate regulatory compliance.
  • US-005: As a retail customer, I want to see real-time status of my onboarding application, so that I know what to expect and when.

Technical Requirements

  • Systems Impacted: Document Upload Service, Application Processing Engine, Notification Service, Admin Dashboard, Audit Logging Service, Customer Portal
  • APIs: POST /api/onboarding/resume, PUT /api/documents/upload, GET /api/onboarding/{id}/status, POST /api/routing/evaluate, GET /api/operations/queue, POST /api/notifications/sla-breach
  • Security: Documents encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3), PII data masked in logs, GDPR right to erasure support

Enterprise Guardrails (Demo Output)

  • [CRITICAL] PII Detected: Customer identity documents → Enforcing AES-256, TLS 1.3, PII masking (GDPR Art. 32)
  • [CRITICAL] Financial Data: Transaction records → Enforcing DORA resilience standards, 99.99% availability (DORA)
  • [HIGH] Automated Decision Making: Risk-based routing → Human-in-the-loop for high-risk, explainability logging (GDPR Art. 22)
  • [HIGH] Data Retention: Onboarding documents → 7-year retention, right to erasure workflow (GDPR Art. 5(1)(e))
  • [MEDIUM] Third-Party Integration: Identity verification → Vendor risk assessment, fallback mechanism
  • [HIGH] Cross-Border Transfer: Customer data → Data residency constraints, SCCs validation (GDPR Ch. V)

  2. SECURITY: PII masked in all logs, error outputs, audit trails

  6. ARCHITECTURE: Document upload as separate microservice
  7. ARCHITECTURE: Append-only audit trail, event sourcing
  8. DATA: Backward-compatible migrations, expand-contract pattern
  9. DATA: 7-year audit retention, 30-day incomplete application expiry
  10. TESTING: >95% unit test coverage for routing logic
